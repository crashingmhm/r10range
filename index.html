Smash factor guide

Smash = ball speed ÷ club speed, so it shows how centred the strike was. Above 1.52 is almost always a misread, so it's marked “check”.

Rating guide

StatGreenAmber

Anything outside amber is red. Carry, total and speeds are judged against your own average with this club, so they need at least 3 shots of it. Bands are typical good-amateur numbers; ask Claude to change any of them.
